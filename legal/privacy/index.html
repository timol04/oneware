Privacy

Controller: Timo Lanter, sole proprietorship Oneware (Switzerland). The Swiss Data Protection Act (FADP) applies.

In short

Oneware has no tracking, no cookies for advertising and no user accounts. Your entries stay in your browser.

What is stored on your device

Your entries (IndexedDB), your license keys, your theme and language choice and backup reminders (localStorage). Your entries and files stay on your device. Only a license key you enter is sent out, to check it (see License check).

Hosting

The website is served as static files by GitHub Pages. When you open a page, the hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address in server logs, also in the USA.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller and processes your payment and billing data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

License check

When you enter a license key, your browser sends the key and a device name to Lemon Squeezy (USA) to activate it, which also shows them your IP address. Afterwards the key is checked again from time to time. If you are offline, the tool keeps working. We do not store the key on a server of our own.

Cloud folder and files

If you connect a cloud folder, backups are written to a folder on your device that your own cloud app syncs. We have no access. Files you open in a tool such as the converter, the PDF toolkit or Verifyxr are processed in your browser and are not uploaded.

Purposes, recipients and countries

We process data only to deliver the website, to sell and activate licenses and to answer your e-mails. Recipients are GitHub (hosting) and Lemon Squeezy (payment and license check), both in the USA, where data protection can differ from Switzerland. Transfers rely on the providers' certification under the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses. E-mails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer them and for legal retention duties.
