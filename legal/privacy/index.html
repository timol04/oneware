Privacy

Controller: Timo Lanter, sole proprietorship Oneware (Switzerland). The Swiss Data Protection Act (FADP) applies.

In short

Oneware sets no cookies, has no personal tracking, no advertising and no user accounts. We count visits anonymously (see Visitor statistics). Your entries stay in your browser.

What is stored on your device

Your entries (IndexedDB), your license keys, your theme and language choice and backup reminders (localStorage). Your entries and files stay on your device. Only a license key you enter is sent out, to check it (see License check). When you continue with a file in another Oneware tool ("Continue with ..."), the file is kept for a short time in the same browser storage on your device until the next tool has picked it up, and at most for one hour. It is never sent anywhere. So that the tools also open without a connection, your browser keeps a copy of the site files (programs and texts, no personal data) in its cache; you can delete it any time in your browser settings. If you scan a receipt in Expenses the photo is read on your device by a text-recognition program that is part of the site; the photo is not saved and not sent anywhere, only the values you confirm are stored as a booking. The program (about 7 MB) is loaded from this site the first time you use the scan.

Hosting

The website is served as static files by GitHub Pages. When you open a page, the hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address in server logs, also in the USA. Links to other sites, such as our YouTube channel, are plain links: nothing is loaded from them until you click.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller and processes your payment and billing data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

What we see about a purchase

When you buy a license, we see the following in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard: order number and time, product and amount, the VAT included, your name and e-mail address, the license key with its status (active or deactivated) and number of activations (for example 1 of 2), and a timeline of the order (order created, receipt e-mailed). We use this data only to give support, handle refunds and manage licenses, for example to answer a question about your purchase, to deactivate a license key or to check whether a refund is possible. Under the Swiss Data Protection Act the basis is performing (or preparing) the purchase contract and our legitimate interest in giving support and preventing misuse of licenses. We do not see your card or other payment details, those stay with Lemon Squeezy. Retention: Lemon Squeezy decides how long it keeps the data on its side. We do not keep a copy of customer data of our own outside the dashboard. We look at the data only as long as needed for support, refunds and legal retention duties, and do not keep it indefinitely.

License check

When you enter a license key, your browser sends the key and a device name to Lemon Squeezy (USA) to activate it, which also shows them your IP address. Afterwards the key is checked again about once a day while you are online. If you are offline, the tool keeps working. We do not store the key on a server of our own.

Cloud folder and files

If you connect a cloud folder, backups are written to a folder on your device that your own cloud app syncs. We have no access. Files you open in a tool such as the converter, the PDF toolkit or Verifyxr are processed in your browser and are not uploaded.

Purposes, recipients and countries

We process data only to deliver the website, to count visits anonymously, to sell and activate licenses and to answer your e-mails. Recipients are GitHub (hosting), Cloudflare (visitor statistics) and Lemon Squeezy (payment and license check), all in the USA, where data protection can differ from Switzerland. Transfers rely on the providers' certification under the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses. E-mails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer them and for legal retention duties.

Visitor statistics

To see how many people visit the site and which pages they open, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics on the live website. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not follow you across sites or build a profile of you. Cloudflare receives the technical request data (such as the page, referrer, browser type, country and your IP address, which is not stored in the statistics) and shows us only totals. We do not see who you are. This applies to page visits only: what you enter or open in a tool is never part of it. If your browser sends "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control, the counter is not loaded at all.
