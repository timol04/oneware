How it works

Oneware tools run entirely in your browser. There is no account and no server that stores your data.

Try it free

Every tool can be used without signing up. Free tools or free limits are listed under "About this tool" on each tool page.

Pay once

If a tool helps you, unlock Premium with a single payment. There is no subscription and no renewal. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy.

Your license key

After the purchase you receive an e-mail with your license key. Open the tool, choose "Enter license key" in the menu and paste it. The key is checked on your device.

A key unlocks one tool in the browser where you enter it. You may use it on your own devices; enter it once per browser.

Your data stays local

Your entries are stored in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. The flip side: if you clear your browser data or switch devices, the data does not follow you automatically. That is what backups are for.

Backups and new devices

  1. On the old device open the tool menu and choose "Export backup". You get a file.
  2. On the new device open the same tool, choose "Import backup" and select the file.
  3. Enter your license key again on the new device.

FAQ

I lost my license key.

Search your inbox for the purchase e-mail from Lemon Squeezy. If you cannot find it, contact us: oneware.contact@gmail.com.

I cleared my browser data. Are my entries gone?

Yes, unless you exported a backup. Import the backup file and enter your license key again.
